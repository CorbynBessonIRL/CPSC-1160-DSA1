Error:
   size = nullptr is wrong since the size of empty array should be 0;

   no delete in copy assign operator which causes some memory leaks

Test:

test 1: copy constructor
    quiz8 obj1(5, 10);
    quiz8 obj2 = obj1;

    obj1 (original): 10 10 10 10 10 
    obj2 (copy constructor): 10 10 10 10 10


test 2: copy assignment operator
    quiz8 obj1(5, 10);  
    quiz8 obj2(3, 20);
    obj2 = obj1;

    obj2 (before assignment): 20 20 20 
    obj2 (after assignment from obj1): 10 10 10
